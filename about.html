Who We Are

Comfort, Restored and Reimagined

Innocent Recliner & Sofa supplies high-quality recliner sofas, refurbishes old sofas, and offers professional repair services across Kenya. What started as a small upholstery workshop has grown into a trusted name for new furniture and furniture care alike.

Every sofa that leaves our workshop — new or restored — is built on the same standard: strong frames, quality foam, and fabric or leather that lasts.

  • Commitment to Quality

    We use durable frames, high-density foam, and premium fabrics and leathers in every piece.

  • Skilled Workmanship

    Our craftsmen bring years of hands-on experience to every repair and refurbishing job.

  • Affordable Pricing

    Honest, transparent quotes with no hidden costs — quality furniture care within reach.

  • Customer Satisfaction

    We stand behind our work, and our repeat customers are proof of it.
